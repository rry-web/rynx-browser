Fluffy pancakes

Makes 8 · Prep 10 min · Cook 15 min

Ingredients

  • 200g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • A pinch of salt
  • 250ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 30g butter, melted, plus a little for the pan

Method

  1. Put the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a large bowl and stir them together.
  2. In a jug, whisk the milk, egg and melted butter until smooth.
  3. Pour the milk mixture into the bowl and stir until only just combined. A few lumps are fine: stirring more makes the pancakes tough.
  4. Heat a frying pan over a medium heat and brush it with a little butter.
  5. Pour in a small ladle of batter for each pancake. Cook for about 2 minutes, until bubbles pop on top and the edges look dry.
  6. Flip each pancake and cook for 1 minute more, until golden underneath.
  7. Keep them warm while you cook the rest, then serve in a stack with butter and syrup.
